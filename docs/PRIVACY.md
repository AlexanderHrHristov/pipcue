# PiPSub Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PiPSub is designed with privacy in mind.

## Data Collection

PiPSub does not intentionally collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

PiPSub does not require a user account.

PiPSub does not use user information for advertising.

PiPSub does not sell user data.

## Website Access

To provide its Picture-in-Picture subtitle functionality, PiPSub may access video player and subtitle information on supported webpages while the extension is being used.

This access is used only to provide the user-facing Picture-in-Picture subtitle functionality.

## Local Processing

Where technically possible, video and subtitle information required by PiPSub is processed locally within the user's browser.

PiPSub does not intentionally transmit the content of subtitles, course videos, browsing history, or visited URLs to the developer.

## Permissions

Browser permissions requested by PiPSub are used only when necessary to provide its declared functionality.

PiPSub does not use permissions for advertising, profiling, or unrelated tracking.

## Third-Party Websites

PiPSub operates alongside third-party websites such as online learning and video platforms.

Those websites have their own privacy policies and data practices.

PiPSub does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PiPSub's functionality changes or when required by applicable policies or law.

Any material change to PiPSub's data practices will be reflected in this policy.
