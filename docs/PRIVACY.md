# PiPCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PiPCue is designed with privacy in mind.

## Data Collection

PiPCue does not intentionally collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

PiPCue does not require a user account.

PiPCue does not use user information for advertising.

PiPCue does not sell user data.

## Website Access

To provide its Picture-in-Picture subtitle functionality, PiPCue may access video player and subtitle information on supported webpages while the extension is being used.

This access is used only to provide the user-facing Picture-in-Picture subtitle functionality.

## Local Processing

Where technically possible, video and subtitle information required by PiPCue is processed locally within the user's browser.

PiPCue does not intentionally transmit the content of subtitles, course videos, browsing history, or visited URLs to the developer.

## Permissions

Browser permissions requested by PiPCue are used only when necessary to provide its declared functionality.

PiPCue does not use permissions for advertising, profiling, or unrelated tracking.

## Third-Party Websites

PiPCue operates alongside third-party websites such as online learning and video platforms.

Those websites have their own privacy policies and data practices.

PiPCue does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PiPCue's functionality changes or when required by applicable policies or law.

Any material change to PiPCue's data practices will be reflected in this policy.
