# PiPSub — Help & Quick Start

## What is PiPSub?

PiPSub is a browser extension designed primarily to solve a common problem with online learning:

**subtitles disappearing when a video is moved into Picture-in-Picture mode.**

PiPSub was originally created for Udemy learners who want to watch courses in a floating Picture-in-Picture window while continuing to see the course subtitles.

Support for additional compatible video platforms may also be available.

---

## Quick Start

### 1. Open your course

Open a supported video lesson in your browser.
PiPSub is optimized primarily for Udemy.

### 2. Enable subtitles

Turn on subtitles or closed captions in the video player.
PiPSub uses the subtitles available from the video or learning platform.

### 3. Start Picture-in-Picture

Use PiPSub to open the video in Picture-in-Picture mode.

The video will appear in a floating window that can remain visible while you work in another application or browser tab.

### 4. Continue learning

When supported by the website and browser, PiPSub keeps the subtitles visible together with the Picture-in-Picture video.
You can now:
- take notes while watching a course;
- work in another application;
- read documentation;
- practice coding;
- follow a tutorial;
- or keep the lesson visible while using another browser tab.

---

## Designed for Udemy

PiPSub was created primarily to improve the Picture-in-Picture experience for Udemy courses.
Standard Picture-in-Picture behavior may not preserve the subtitles displayed by the Udemy player.
PiPSub is designed to bridge that gap and make Picture-in-Picture more useful for learners who rely on subtitles.

---

## Other Websites

PiPSub may also work with compatible HTML5 video players on other websites.

Compatibility depends on:

- the website's video player;
- how subtitles are provided;
- browser security restrictions;
- and changes made by the website.

Udemy remains PiPSub's primary supported use case.

---

## Troubleshooting

### The Picture-in-Picture window opens but there are no subtitles

Make sure subtitles are enabled in the original video player before starting PiPSub.

### PiPSub does not detect the video

Reload the page and start playing the video before trying PiPSub again.

### Subtitles stop appearing

Close Picture-in-Picture, confirm that subtitles are still visible in the original player, and start PiPSub again.

### PiPSub does not work on a particular website

Some websites use custom players, embedded frames or browser restrictions that prevent PiPSub from accessing the video or subtitle information.
Compatibility with every website cannot be guaranteed.
