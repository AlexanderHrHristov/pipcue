Privacy Policy for PiPCue

Effective date: August 23, 2026
Last updated: August 23, 2026

PiPCue is a browser extension that displays synchronized subtitles and playback controls inside a Picture-in-Picture video window. It is designed primarily for Udemy and also supports compatible video players on selected websites.

Summary

PiPCue is privacy-friendly and operates locally in the user's browser.

PiPCue does not collect personal information.

PiPCue does not collect browsing history.

PiPCue does not transmit subtitles or video content.

PiPCue does not use analytics, telemetry or advertising.

PiPCue does not sell or share user data.

PiPCue does not require an account or registration.

PiPCue does not use remote code.

Information Processed Locally

To provide its core functionality, PiPCue temporarily accesses the active video's media element, subtitle tracks and visible caption text on supported websites.

This information is processed only inside the user's browser for the purpose of displaying synchronized subtitles and playback controls in the Picture-in-Picture window. It is not collected, stored on external servers or transmitted to the developer or third parties.

Recent caption history, when displayed by the extension, exists only temporarily in memory while the Picture-in-Picture window is active. It is cleared when the PiP session ends or the page is closed or reloaded.

Data Collection

PiPCue does not collect, store or transmit:

names, email addresses or other personally identifiable information;

authentication information, passwords or account credentials;

financial or payment information;

location information or IP addresses;

browsing history;

user activity, clicks, keystrokes or mouse movements;

website content, subtitles, audio or video;

health information or personal communications.

Permissions and Website Access

PiPCue runs only on the supported website patterns declared in the extension manifest. This access is required to identify the active video, read the currently displayed subtitles and create the Picture-in-Picture interface.

PiPCue does not use this access for tracking, profiling, advertising or any purpose unrelated to its core Picture-in-Picture subtitle functionality.

Local Storage and Retention

PiPCue does not maintain a remote database and does not retain user data on developer-controlled servers.

Temporary video state and caption history used during a PiP session remain in browser memory only for as long as needed to provide the requested functionality.

Third-Party Services

PiPCue does not integrate analytics, advertising networks or third-party tracking services.

Supported websites operate independently and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. PiPCue is not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

Data Sharing and Sale

PiPCue does not sell, rent, share or transfer user data to third parties.

Children's Privacy

PiPCue does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users because the extension does not collect personal information.

Security

PiPCue minimizes privacy and security risks by processing the information required for its functionality locally in the browser and by not operating a remote data-collection service.

Changes to This Policy

If PiPCue's functionality or data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated before the relevant extension version is published. The effective date and last updated date at the top of this document will also be revised.
